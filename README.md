# temploracraft Fellowship

React, taught in two days, with a graded assignment and a route to an
internship at the end.

Everything in the room runs in the browser. **No installs, no accounts,
no "works on my machine".** Click a link, it opens, you fix something.

---

## The exercises

Eight minutes each. Open the link, read the comment at the top of
`src/App.tsx`, fix it. The answer is in each exercise's README behind
a toggle — use it if you are stuck, but try first.

## The course

The workshop is six hours. It buys you the map. The journey is about
twenty hours and it is in [notes/](./notes).

Finish those eight modules and you can hold your own in a frontend
interview. Not because of a certificate — because you will have built
the thing eight times.

---

## What happens after

| Stage | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Workshop | Everyone |
| Assignment | Everyone who attended |
| Online cohort, 5 weeks | 6–8 selected on the scorecard |
| Interview | 2–4 |
| Paid internship | 1–2 |

Roughly one in twenty attendees reaches an interview. Worth knowing up
front rather than in March.
